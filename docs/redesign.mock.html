Direction A: Apple Maps, tidied

Same look as now. One sidebar with search, a view picker and a "Now" list. One stack of map buttons on the right. Layers, legend and feed health live behind the Layers button. Every card has the same order.

Try it: pick a view, open Layers, press (i) on a layer, click a row marked "full card", press Follow on the aircraft, open the gear. The map picture is the Overview layers as the app draws them today. It does not change when you switch views here, and it still shows today's colours.

What I need from you

  1. Which direction should we build: A, B or C?
    Recommendation: A.
  2. Do the nine groups make sense? Is there any layer you would move, merge or drop?
    Recommendation: keep them as shown, with Sanctioned under Sea and Heads of state under Sites.
  3. Which view should open at start: Overview, the view you last used, or one you pick?
    Recommendation: Overview at start, with the camera where you left off.
  4. Should red be kept for "Now" only, and should airspace warnings become outlines instead of pink areas?
    Recommendation: yes to both. News circles become neutral grey-blue.
  5. Can the floating legend and the feeds card go away, with each layer's symbols and health shown in the Layers panel and the full feed list in Settings?
    Recommendation: yes. A small Key button shows the symbols of what is on screen.
  6. Which settings do you want at all? The ones marked "real need" in the sheet are my suggestion for the first build.
    Recommendation: build the "real need" ones first. Leave the others out unless you ask.
  7. Should low-power mode switch on by itself when the Mac runs on battery?
    Recommendation: off by default, with a switch. Safari does not tell a page reliably whether it is on battery.

Build order

  1. The Layers panel: groups, sub-options, the ground-picture choice, counts and ages, the new colours and airspace outlines. This calms the screen the most.
  2. Views, the opening view and the Settings sheet with feed health and keys. The feeds card goes away.
  3. The shared card layout, first for aircraft, satellites, news and bases, then every other layer one by one.
  4. The "Now" list sections, search across layers, the Key button, the control stack, label rules and low-power mode.
