World Watch · Footprint · colour

Footprint colours, taken from real pictures

The shape stays exactly as you approved it. The blue on white looked generated because it is interface blue, the same blue every web app uses, on a cartoon-white eyeball. Here every colour comes from something real: pixels from two satellite pictures, a declassified spy-satellite frame, or the measured paint chips of U.S. military aircraft. The colour groups were found by clustering each picture's pixels; each swatch is the middle pixel of its group, so it is a colour that is really in the picture.

My pick

Two satellites

The globe in the colour of the ocean as NASA's deep-space camera saw it; the footprint in the colour of the fields Sentinel-2 photographed around Munich; the pupil in Munich's forest.

  • Space#000000EPIC's own background around the disc
  • Ocean#2F3C4Ddarkest of 7 colour groups on the disc, 13 % of it: open ocean
  • Fields#837F5Elightest of 7 groups in the Munich scene, 6 %: harvested fields
  • Forest#101A06darkest group in the Munich scene, 13 %: forest in shadow
  • Cloud#B9BCBBlightest group on the EPIC disc, 10 %: cloud tops
  • Haze#7C7E81EPIC group, 17 %: thin cloud over sea

#11161C and #ECEDEC are steps between sampled colours for panels and the light background; #484F57 is an EPIC group.

At real size

In use

What works

The richest and the most World Watch: two real satellites in one mark, far and near, and home in the middle. Muted, physical colours that no UI kit ships with. The olive footprint keeps the eye from looking like a cartoon.

What does not

Dark: on a dark taskbar the blue globe sits close to the black plate at 16 px (still visible, less punchy). The olive can read as khaki or military.

Recommendation

Two satellites

It keeps the story of the mark (a satellite looking at Munich) and puts it into the colour: the Earth as NASA's deep-space camera saw it, the footprint as Sentinel-2 saw the fields around Munich. It is the only option with real colour that does not look like a template, and it reads at taskbar size. Whole Earth is the calmer version of the same idea; Compass Ghost is the choice if you want it to feel like defence hardware.
