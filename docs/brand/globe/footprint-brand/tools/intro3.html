Footprint in real brand colours

Every colour here is copied from a real brand: NASA's and NATO's published guidelines, Apple's system colours from its design guidelines, and the colours inside Telegram's and Signal's own logo files and code. Nothing is mixed or sampled. The shape is unchanged.

What the guidelines say, and what went wrong last time:

  • Apple, app icons: Avoid using black for your icon's background. Lighten a black background so the icon doesn't blend into the display background. Most of the dull options had black tiles.
  • Apple, app icons: Design a background that both stands out and emphasizes foreground content, and prefer a simple background, such as a solid color or gradient. So: one strong brand colour, and white for the subject.
  • Web accessibility rule for shapes (WCAG 2.1, 1.4.11): parts of a graphic need at least 3:1 contrast. The ocean globe on black last round was 1.8:1, which is why it looked muddy. Each option below lists its contrast; numbers under 3 are dotted.
