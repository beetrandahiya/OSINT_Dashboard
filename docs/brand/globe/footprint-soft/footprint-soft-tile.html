World Watch · Footprint · soft tile

Footprint on a soft lavender and blue tile

The Anduril version you liked, black Earth (#010101), white footprint and black point on Munich, with the lime swapped for a very light lavender-to-blue gradient. The tile colours are steps of the Tailwind CSS colour scale, one of the most used published design palettes (colors.js, v3.4.17). The 3D comes from soft light from above, a little shade along the bottom, a thin light rim, and a sheen and contact shadow on the globe so it sits on the tile like a ball.

My pick

Two soft pools

Lavender (violet-300) glowing from the top left and light blue (sky-200) from the bottom right, over a pale indigo-100 base, with light from above and a little shade at the bottom. The most 3D of the four.

Recommendation

Two soft pools

Both colours show and the tile has real depth without getting loud. The black Earth stays the strongest thing at every size, which is what makes it work on any taskbar. At 16 and 24 px the tile reads as near white with a hint of colour: the small sizes keep the tile simple on purpose.
