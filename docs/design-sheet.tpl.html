World Watch design sheet

Every icon, marker, colour and component the app uses today, taken from its code on 14 November 2026. Each specimen is shown with the light tokens on the left and the dark tokens on the right.

Aircraft icons

Drawn by me as paths in the code; none are Apple's. Top: enlarged. Below each: the real sizes on the map at world zoom, country zoom and city zoom. Civil aircraft are indigo, military purple; the shape follows the category the aircraft broadcasts.

Map markers

Circles carry a short label inside, as Apple's map guidance allows. Size means something only where stated.

Layer colours

One colour per layer, from Apple's system colours, with a dark-mode partner. The same colour is used for the chip, the map marker and the list badge.

Interface colours and materials

Text, separators, fills and the panel material. Values measured from maps.apple.com's stylesheets.

Type

The system font (SF Pro on a Mac; this page shows your system's own). Sizes follow Apple's guidelines; nothing is smaller than 11.

Components

The sidebar, a detail card, the feeds pill and the bottom strip, over a stand-in for the map so the frosted panels have something to blur.

Shape and motion

Fixed numbers used everywhere.

Not Apple's and open to change: every aircraft shape, the letters inside markers (stand-ins until SF Symbols are made on your Mac), the 380 px sidebar (Apple uses 420), and the choice of which system colour goes to which layer.
